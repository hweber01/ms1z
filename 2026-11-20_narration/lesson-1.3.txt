Lesson 1.3: Exercise: Task or Goal?

In this exercise you'll practice the first idea of the course: a task is not a goal.

Download the worksheet from the resources of this lesson.

In part one, you'll read ten sentences and decide whether each one is a goal or a task.

Remember: a goal is the end state, what has to be true when you're done. A task is an action, one way to get there.

In part two, you'll take your own to-do list and ask one question about every item: which goal does this serve?

It takes about ten minutes. When you're done, check your answers with the answer key.
