Lesson 1.2: How This Course Works

Before we go further, here's how this course works, so you can get the most out of it.

Every lesson is short and covers one idea.

If you have ten minutes, you can finish a lesson and its exercise.

After most lessons there's an exercise. Some take two minutes. Some take twenty.

The exercises are where the method stops being an idea and becomes a habit. Please don't skip them.

We'll start with simple, everyday examples, like getting lunch on the table.

Then we'll move to work. Later you'll run a restaurant kitchen on a busy night, and at the end, a football tournament.

Each example is a little more complex than the one before.

With this lesson you'll find a workbook to download. It has every worksheet and template in the course.

Print it, or keep it open on your screen, and write your answers down. Writing forces you to be precise.

One more thing. Think of a real challenge from your work right now. A project, an assignment, a team problem.

Keep it in mind as we go. By the end of the course, you'll plan it from start to finish.

Let's start. Your first exercise is next.
